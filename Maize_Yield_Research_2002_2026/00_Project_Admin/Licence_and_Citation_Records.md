# Licence and Citation Records

This repository strictly abides by open-data principles, academic citation standards, and data distribution agreements.

## 1. USDA National Agricultural Statistics Service (NASS)
- **Datasets**: QuickStats Corn Grain Yield, Crop Progress & Condition, Fertilizer Chemical Survey, Cropland Data Layer (CDL).
- **Licence**: Public Domain (United States Government Work, 17 U.S.C. § 105). Open data for commercial and non-commercial academic research without restrictions.
- **Citation**:
  > United States Department of Agriculture, National Agricultural Statistics Service (USDA NASS). (2026). *Quick Stats Agricultural Database*. Retrieved November 2026, from https://quickstats.nass.usda.gov/
  > Boryan, C., Yang, Z., Mueller, R., & Craig, M. (2011). Monitoring US agriculture: the US Department of Agriculture, National Agricultural Statistics Service, Cropland Data Layer program. *Geocarto International*, 26(5), 341-358.

## 2. PRISM Climate Group
- **Dataset**: PRISM 4km Gridded Monthly & Annual Datasets (AN81m/AN81d: ppt, tmin, tmax, tmean).
- **Licence**: PRISM Terms of Use. Gridded datasets at 4km resolution are provided free of charge for academic research and non-commercial applications. Redistribution must retain PRISM provenance.
- **Citation**:
  > PRISM Climate Group, Oregon State University. (2026). *PRISM Gridded Climate Data*. Retrieved November 2026, from https://prism.oregonstate.edu/
  > Daly, C., Halbleib, M., Smith, J. I., Gibson, W. P., Doggett, M. K., Taylor, G. H., Curtis, J., & Pasteris, P. P. (2008). Physiographically sensitive mapping of climatological temperature and precipitation across the conterminous United States. *International Journal of Climatology*, 28(15), 2031-2064.
