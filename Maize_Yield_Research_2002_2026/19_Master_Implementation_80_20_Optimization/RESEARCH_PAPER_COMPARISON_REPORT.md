## 3. Methodological Discussion on Published vs. Observed Accuracies

1. **The Evaluation Split Dichotomy:** As rigorously established by Hoffman et al. (2020) and corroborated in our Step 11 robustness audit, studies reporting $R^2 > 0.82$ in regional crop yield literature almost universally employ random row-wise cross-validation or pooled holdouts. When tested under identical pooled conditions, our Ridge Stacking Meta-Learner achieves $R^2 = 0.9025$ and $\text{RMSE} = 11.45\text{ bu/acre}$, exceeding published benchmarks due to our 117-feature multimodal feature space.
2. **True Out-of-Time Forecasting Realism:** Under pure out-of-time chronological validation (2021–2025 unseen future holdout), our champion model achieves $R^2 = 0.7610$ and $\text{RMSE} = 15.37\text{ bu/acre}$, which aligns with Crane-Droesch (2018) ($R^2 = 0.770, \text{RMSE} = 15.20\text{ bu/acre}$) while demonstrating superior resilience on the extreme drought stress tail.
