## 3. Detailed Analysis of 2026 Coverage
- **Corn Grain Yield**: NASS produces final county yield estimates using farmer survey responses collected throughout late autumn and winter. The 2026 county estimates are officially published in late February 2027. Hence, 2026 is designated as **Unpublished Ground Truth**.
- **NOAA NCEI Climate**: Complete monthly county series available through October 2026. Months 11 and 12 are pending calendar progression.
- **PRISM Climate Grids**: Annual grid for 2026 does not yet exist. In accordance with project instructions, provisional monthly grids for May through October 2026 (the complete corn growing season) were downloaded and unpacked.
- **USDA RMA Insurance**: The 2026 summary file (`sobcov_2026.zip`) reflects policies earning premium and early indemnities. Final claim adjustments continue through spring 2027.
- **USDA CDL**: CDL 2026 raster processing requires full post-season satellite compositing and is released in February 2027.
